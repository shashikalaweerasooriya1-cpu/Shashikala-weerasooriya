About the teacher

Meet Shashikala Weerasooriya

Shashikala Weerasooriya teaches English to nursery and primary school children, with a focus on spoken English and everyday language development. Her classes are built around friendly, engaging methods that help young learners find their voice and grow in confidence, one lesson at a time. She currently teaches at an international school alongside her class groups in Kadawatha, Kelaniya and Kiribathgoda.
